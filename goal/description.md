# Research Goal & Scope: Cross-Continental Descriptive & Structural Analysis of 9 Elite Technical Universities (US vs. Europe vs. Asia)

## 1. Objective
Conduct a rigorous, multi-dimensional descriptive and structural comparative analysis across **9 leading technical universities** spanning three global regions ($3 \times 3$ regional matrix):
- **United States (`US`)**:
  1. **MIT** (Massachusetts Institute of Technology, Cambridge, MA)
  2. **Caltech** (California Institute of Technology, Pasadena, CA)
  3. **Georgia Tech** (Georgia Institute of Technology, Atlanta, GA)
- **Europe (`EU`)**:
  4. **Imperial College London** (United Kingdom)
  5. **ETH Zurich** (Swiss Federal Institute of Technology, Switzerland)
  6. **TUM** (Technical University of Munich, Germany)
- **Asia (`ASIA`)**:
  7. **Tsinghua University** (Beijing, China)
  8. **KAIST** (Korea Advanced Institute of Science and Technology, Daejeon, South Korea)
  9. **NTU Singapore** (Nanyang Technological University, Singapore)

---

## 2. Core Comparative Dimensions
1. **Raw vs. Corrected Student-Facing Budgets (3-Tier Accounting Boundary)**:
   - **Tier 0 (Raw Consolidated Budget)**: Official headline institutional operating expenditure/revenue in local currency, Nominal USD, and OECD/World Bank PPP-adjusted USD.
   - **Tier 1 (Core Academic Budget ex-Hospitals & Defense FFRDCs)**: Strips non-student clinical hospital turnover (e.g., TUM *Klinikum rechts der Isar*, Imperial NHS clinical pass-throughs) and closed defense/aerospace Federally Funded Research & Development Centers (MIT *Lincoln Laboratory*, Caltech *Jet Propulsion Laboratory [JPL]*, Georgia Tech *GTRI*).
   - **Tier 2 (Ecosystem-Augmented Student-Facing Budget)**: Incorporates the academically integrated share of co-located external research institutes whose joint professors (*Doppelberufungen*), laboratories, and research groups directly supervise Bachelor, Master, and Doctoral students—specifically including **Max Planck Institutes (IPP, MPE, MPA, MPQ) and Helmholtz Munich / ESO / FRM II in Garching** for TUM, the **ETH Domain** (PSI, Empa, Eawag, WSL) for ETH Zurich, **Broad & Whitehead Institutes** for MIT, **Francis Crick / affiliated centres** for Imperial, **IBS / Daedeok Innopolis** for KAIST, **A\*STAR co-located institutes** for NTU, and **National Laboratories** for Tsinghua.
2. **Student Enrollment Scale & Denominator Harmonization**:
   - Contrasts **Raw Enrolled Headcount** against **Standardized On-Campus Full-Time Equivalent (FTE)** students (correcting for Georgia Tech's $>20{,}000$ online OMSCS/OMSA cohort and harmonizing undergraduate, Master's, and doctoral student populations).
3. **Global Rankings, Multi-Dimensional Research Efficiency & Employability**:
   - **QS World University Rankings** (Overall & Engineering/Technology Subject Rank).
   - **Emerging Global Employability University Ranking (GEURS)** and **QS Employer Reputation**.
   - **Multi-Dimensional Research Efficiency**: Field-normalized citation excellence (**CWTS Leiden $PP_{\text{top 10\%}}$ and $PP_{\text{top 1\%}}$**), **QS Citations per Faculty**, high-impact publication yield per $\$10\text{M}$ PPP research expenditure, and deep-tech spin-off/patent intensity.
4. **International Master's Student Lifecycle (Tuition, Living Costs & 10-Year Salary ROI)**:
   - Evaluates the financial lifecycle for a **Non-Domestic / Non-EU STEM Master's Student** (anchored on Engineering & Computer Science / EECS / MechEng, with sensitivity bounds from traditional Engineering to CS/AI).
   - Accounts for **1-year** (Imperial, NTU) vs. **2-year** (MIT, Caltech, Georgia Tech, ETH, TUM, Tsinghua, KAIST) degree durations, recent fee reforms (TUM non-EU tuition fees introduced 2024/25; ETH 2025 international fee revision), living costs, scholarship/TA/RA/HiWi offsets, and **10-year cumulative post-graduation earnings** (Nominal USD Gross, After-Tax Net, and PPP-Adjusted).
